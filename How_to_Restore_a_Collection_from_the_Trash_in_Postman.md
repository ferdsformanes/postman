# How to Restore a Collection from the Trash in Postman

If you accidentally delete a collection, Postman stores it in the
**Trash** for a limited time, depending on your account plan.

## Step 1: Open the Trash

Navigate to the bottom-right corner of the Postman interface and click
the **Status Bar / Footer**.

Click **Tools**, then select **Trash** (or click **Trash** directly if
it is already visible).

## Step 2: Locate the Deleted Collection

Browse the list of deleted items and find the collection you want to
restore.

## Step 3: Restore the Collection

Hover over the deleted collection.

Click the **Restore** arrow icon.

## Step 4: Choose the Destination Workspace

Select the workspace where you want the collection to be restored.

Click **Restore Collection**.

## That's It!

Your collection has been restored and is ready to use in the selected
workspace.
