How to Export a Postman Environment (Cisco SD-WAN Example)

Step 1

Open Postman and go to the Environments panel.

Step 2

Locate your Cisco SD-WAN Sandbox environment.

Step 3

Click the three dots (⋮) next to the environment name.

Step 4

Select Export.

Step 5

Choose a location on your computer and save the file, for example:

Cisco_SDWAN_Sandbox_Environment.json

Step 6

Verify the exported environment by importing the JSON file into Postman.
You should see your environment variables, such as: - base_url -
username - password

Done! Your Cisco SD-WAN Postman environment has been exported and is
ready to back up, share, or import into another Postman workspace.
