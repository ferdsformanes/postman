# How to Import Collections and Environments in Postman

## Import a Collection

### Step 1

Open Postman.

### Step 2

Click the **Import** button in the upper-left corner.

### Step 3

Click **Files**.

### Step 4

Select the exported collection JSON file.

### Step 5

Click **Open**.

### Step 6

Verify that the collection appears in the **Collections** panel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Import an Environment

### Step 1

Click the **Import** button in the upper-left corner.

### Step 2

Click **Files**.

### Step 3

Select the exported environment JSON file.

### Step 4

Click **Open**.

### Step 5

Verify that the environment appears in the **Environments** panel.

### Step 6

Select the imported environment from the **Environment** drop-down list.

### Step 7

If the imported environment does not contain variable values, enter the
required values (such as **base_url**, **username**, and **password**)
in the environment.

> **Note:** In Postman v12, exported environment files may not include
> variable values. If the values are blank after importing, simply enter
> them again before running your requests.
