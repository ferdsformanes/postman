# How to Export Collections and Environments in Postman

## Export a Collection

### Step 1

In the **Collections** panel, locate the collection you want to export.

### Step 2

Click the **three dots (⋮)** next to the collection name.

### Step 3

Select **Export**.

### Step 4

Choose the **Collection Format** (the default option is recommended).

### Step 5

Click **Export**.

### Step 6

Choose a folder on your computer and click **Save**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Export an Environment

### Step 1

In the **Environments** panel, locate the environment you want to
export.

### Step 2

If you want the variable values included in the exported JSON file, make
sure the values are copied to the **Shared (Initial) Value** column or
marked as **Shared** in the Postman UI before exporting.

> **Note:** If the values remain as local or secret values, they may not
> be included in the exported environment file.

### Step 3

Click the **three dots (⋮)** next to the environment name.

### Step 4

Select **Export**.

### Step 5

Choose a location on your computer.

### Step 6

Click **Save**.
